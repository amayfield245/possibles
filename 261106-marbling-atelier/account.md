# Possible #261106: The Marbling Atelier

Concept · made 6 Nov 2026 by Antony Mayfield with Fable, from one prompt · [open the atelier](https://claude.ai/artifact/HEHmnKshs9KtKKsZUGnfGK) · film: `out/possible-261106-marbling-atelier.mp4`

## What

From one prompt, Fable researched and built an app that explores nine centuries of the craft of paper marbling. You float colours on a bath, comb them through, lay down a sheet and see it bound. Its pattern book holds 26 patterns from Japan, Persia, Istanbul and Europe, each with its names, place and date, and it works each one on the bath, pass by pass. Under it runs the maths of a 2012 paper.

## So what

In a real bath nothing can be undone: a bath gives one good print. Here every stroke can be. Its opening Bouquet takes 9 passes and 1,481 strokes, and the history runs them back to a clean bath. What it taught me: an app made from one prompt can be rich enough to need exploring in its own right. Far from "single-use software".

## What next

I made it as a test. I keep going back, because it is beautiful and I keep learning. Is the history right? No marbler has checked it yet.
