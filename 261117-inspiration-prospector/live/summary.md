# Inspiration prospector session

**Problem:** How could a professional services firm grow when AI does the drafting?

19 Nov 2026 · 43 ideas · 8 dimensions

Every score here is a model judgement, not a measurement.

## Axes

- X: Order ↔ Chaos
- Y: Function ↔ Emotion
- Z: Predictable ↔ Surprising

## Dimensions

- Order ↔ Chaos (Hive): How rule-bound or unruly the idea is.
- Function ↔ Emotion (Hive): Whether it earns its place by use or by feeling.
- Predictable ↔ Surprising (Hive): How far it departs from what people expect.
- Mainstream ↔ Niche (Hive): How many people it is for.
- Guild workshop ↔ Factory line (Claude): Whether growth comes from medieval-style masters and apprentices or from industrial-scale standard output.
- Bauhaus plain ↔ Baroque flourish (Claude): Whether clients pay for stripped-back clarity or for rich, ornate, visibly hand-finished work.
- Formula One pit ↔ Michelin kitchen (Claude): Borrow speed and telemetry from racing teams, or taste and signature from top restaurants.
- Silent machine ↔ Signing ceremony (Claude): Whether the firm hides the work or turns sign-off and judgement into a ritual clients value.

## Furthest from the centre

- **Bespoke illuminated contracts** (0.86, 1750s test 6/10): Hire calligraphers and illustrators to turn key agreements into handmade keepsakes that founders frame, celebrating the deal as much as recording it.
- **Invisible compliance layer** (0.86, 1750s test 3/10): Plug the firm's rules into client software so contracts, filings and checks happen in the background with no meetings at all.
- **Midnight contract vending machine** (0.83, 1750s test 3/10): Clients answer ten questions on a kiosk or app and get a finished, checked document in four minutes, any hour.
- **Fixed price document subscription** (0.83, 1750s test 1/10): Offer unlimited standard documents for a flat monthly fee, produced by AI and checked by a small review team within hours.
- **White label drafting plant** (0.80, 1750s test 2/10): Run a high volume AI drafting operation that smaller firms rent under their own names.

## Gaps

- **Chaos · Emotion · Predictable**: impossible. A firm that is both chaotic and emotional will always surprise people. Predictable chaos stops being chaos, so ideas here cancel themselves out.
- **Chaos · between Function and Emotion · Predictable**: impossible. Disorder that everyone can foresee is just routine with a messy name. Nothing chaotic stays predictable for long in client work.
- **Chaos · Function · Predictable**: unfashionable. Loose, unmanaged freelance pools doing routine work could function, but firms sell reliability and clients distrust anything that looks disorganised.
- **between Order and Chaos · Function · between Predictable and Surprising**: unfashionable. Middling, practical moves with a slight twist get little attention. Talk about AI in firms favours either safe efficiency plays or bold reinventions, so the sensible middle stays empty.
  - Sell the checking, not drafting: Let clients draft with their own AI, then charge a fixed fee to review, correct and stand behind the result with the firm's insurance.
  - Juniors placed inside clients: Since drafting no longer trains juniors, second them into client teams for a fee to learn the business and spot new work early.
  - Weekly client problem sweep: Use the hours saved on drafting to scan each client's contracts, filings and news weekly, then send a short list of issues worth fixing.
- **Chaos · Emotion · between Predictable and Surprising**: unexplored. Loose, feeling-led ideas of middling novelty, such as open house client days or drop-in clinics, could work. Nobody has yet looked here.
- **between Order and Chaos · Function · Predictable**: unexplored. This is the home of ordinary efficiency moves like cross-selling and price cuts. Too obvious to list, so the map skipped them.
- **Order · Emotion · Predictable**: unfashionable. Steady, ritual client care such as birthday calls and annual lunches works well, but it seems old fashioned next to talk of AI.
- **between Order and Chaos · Emotion · Predictable**: unfashionable. Familiar relationship building, like client dinners and friendly check-ins, still wins work. It is seen as dull and hard to measure, so it gets ignored.

## Stretch tests

- **Sell the checking, not drafting** along Order ↔ Chaos: It breaks at both ends: full order takes drafting back from the client, full chaos makes the guarantee uninsurable. The idea needs free drafting but a bounded sign-off.
  - −1.0 Locked template sign-off (breaks)
  - −0.5 Tiered review menu
  - 0.0 Open door review
  - +0.5 Live draft patrol
  - +1.0 Vouch for everything (breaks)
