## What

Every problem has a possibility space. The obvious answers sit where everyone looks, and they are the most competed for. The Inspiration prospector asks Claude to map a problem before listing answers to it. It names dimensions, writes ideas pushed to the far ends of each, scores every idea, and draws the result as a space you can turn. Then it shows the regions with no idea in them.

## So what

We asked it how a professional services firm could grow when AI does the drafting. 12 of 27 regions were empty. None is filled without a verdict: impossible, unfashionable or unexplored. One it called unfashionable. Talk about AI favours safe efficiency or bold reinvention, "so the sensible middle stays empty". Asked to fill it, Claude proposed selling the checking, not the drafting.

Every score is Claude's judgement, not a measurement. That gives a leadership team something to argue with, which is the point of it.
